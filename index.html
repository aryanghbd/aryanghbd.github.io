My work spans the whole 9 yards of the modern AI infrastructure scene, i.e ML infrastructure, backend systems and applied AI: inference, retrieval, distributed systems, model serving, performance engineering and the unpleasant plumbing between all of them. I derive most of my enjoyment from fighting fires on-call, ghosthunting in labyrinthian codebases and being the point guy to fix problems, build and then scale stuff that works well. A pondering code monkey, but a code monkey at heart.

Lately I have been enjoying building greenfield privatised agentic frameworks to serve clients with legacy systems. In the modern AI economy, buzzwords like 'agents', 'multimodal', 'rag' get thrown around like chewed up dog toys. My role is to build, scale, and fix systems that work for clients that operate in rigid regulatory environments and require high privacy, bringing these technological paradigms down to earth in a way that works for them.

Outside of work I enjoy open-source development, digital decentralisation, competitive programming and digital and AI sovereignty.
